Mail DNS Check

Paste domains to check their mail authentication records. Works with any provider, with extra checks for Microsoft 365, Google Workspace and other known services. Lookups run in your browser over DNS-over-HTTPS.

MX

Where mail is delivered, and which provider or gateway handles it.

SPF

One valid record, a safe all, at most 10 lookups, and the include your provider needs.

DKIM

Active keys on 36 common selectors plus any you add. Microsoft 365 CNAMEs checked in detail.

DMARC

One record with p=quarantine or reject and a reporting address.

Overall status is the worst of SPF, DKIM and DMARC. MX only fails a domain when no MX exists. DNS can't list DKIM selectors, so a key under a custom selector only shows up if you add that selector. The Microsoft 365 tenant comes from Microsoft's public sign-in metadata.
